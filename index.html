The problem

A hostel complaint goes into a paper register at the warden's desk. Nobody reads it back. The fan keeps making its noise for a month.

What HostelFix does

You write the problem in your own words — Hindi, English, mixed. HostelFix reads the words, picks the right trade, sets a priority, and pins it on this board.

What changes

The complaint stays on the board where everyone can see it — new, given out, being fixed — until it is marked done. Nothing sinks into a register.

How it works

  1. Write it plainly. "Nal tapak raha hai" is fine. Spelling does not matter, the words do.
  2. The words pick the trade. Pankha, spark and bijli go to the electrician. Nal, paani and naali go to the plumber. Safety words — spark, shock, current — jump the queue.
  3. It stays pinned till done. The board and the block above both show it. When the work is finished, it is marked done in front of everyone.

Honest labels: the routing is keyword matching, done in your browser — no AI model, no server. Every slip shows the words that matched, so a wrong route can be spotted and argued with. If no words match, the slip goes to the warden desk for a person to read.
